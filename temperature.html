Data transparency: Open-Meteo (primary) and MET Norway (backup) serve numerical weather model estimates for temperature 2 metres above ground, not measurements at each neighbourhood. Nearby localities may share the same model grid cell. Pune is represented by one city-level temperature reading, without locality markers. For other cities, neighbourhood data may be available from OpenStreetMap; coverage varies. City coordinates are verified on demand. Open-Meteo temperatures are fetched in batches; backup requests are limited to protect the free service. The state/city directory is a curated list, not a complete census of settlements. No temperatures are fabricated if the service fails. Weather: Open-Meteo · Backup: Norwegian Meteorological Institute (MET Norway), CC BY 4.0 · Base layers: EOX Sentinel-2 2016 (CC BY 4.0) / OpenStreetMap.
Map imagery shows historical Sentinel-2 satellite RGB data (EOX Cloudless 2016, CC BY 4.0). Weather and AQI markers are separate modelled data for geocoded cities, NOT temperatures or pollution measured from the satellite pixels. Use the layer switcher to view streets.
