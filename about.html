How Urban Sentinel works

Understand what is measured, what is estimated, and where professional advice is still essential.

Assessment logic

Building type, area, occupants, roof, floor, sunlight, ventilation and user-selected problem determine a shortlist of actions. Budget estimates and nominal coverage are heuristic allowances, not measured savings or site-specific engineering designs.

We don't claim specific °C reductions. Reports include limitations and recommended next steps.

Weather data

The temperature map uses Open-Meteo modelled outdoor data: temperature 2 metres above ground and apparent temperature. A weather model is not a neighborhood-specific sensor, nor is it a thermal satellite image.

Free API access without an API key is intended for non-commercial use within service limits. Public commercial deployment must confirm licensing or use an appropriately licensed provider.

Map & geographic detail

OpenStreetMap base tiles are shown through Leaflet with required attribution. We use a state-filtered directory of 580 Indian city entries across 36 states and union territories, and up to 20 OSM neighbourhoods loaded per city. It is not a complete directory.

Neighbourhood coordinates are retrieved from OpenStreetMap, city centres from Open-Meteo geocoding. Coverage varies; this is not parcel-level positioning.

Costs and suppliers

All plan prices are example INR allowances, not live offers. Amazon links are search queries, not verified products, affiliations, endorsements or price quotes.

Contact local professionals for structural, waterproofing, electrical, HVAC, occupational safety and fire requirements. The website does not verify contractors.

Your project data

Project saving works without a login and uses this browser only. You can export a backup or download a PDF. Deleting browser data can permanently erase unsaved project records.

Evidence standards

User-entered indoor temperatures are self-reported. Comparisons depend on time of day, outdoor weather, room use and instrument quality. Without a controlled measurement protocol they do not establish causation.

Forecast-model values can fail or be unavailable; the app shows “—” rather than inventing readings.

Startup model · free to plan, verified referrals to execute

Urban Sentinel helps homeowners assess heat-related problems, compare illustrative building improvements and contact suitable independent contractors. Customers do not pay a subscription to use the planning tools.

The proposed income is a negotiated commission on successfully completed, owner-confirmed referred projects. For example, a ₹50,000 project at an illustrative 5% agreed rate would mean ₹2,500 commission. This is an example, not a claim of actual revenue or a rate accepted by the businesses in our public directory.

A partner is presented as confirmed only when the operator has validated business identity and its referral agreement. The website does not process payments.
