Outdoor activity guidance

Select a city to check outdoor conditions

This guidance is based on modelled US AQI. When data is unavailable, we cannot determine whether outdoor conditions are safe.

Air quality can vary by street and change quickly. Follow official local advisories, especially during smoke or pollution events.

About this data: AQI values come from the Open-Meteo Air Quality API, using the US AQI scale and CAMS modelled data. They are not real-time ground sensor measurements, not India's CPCB AQI, and not medical clearance to go outdoors. The AQI model has limited spatial resolution; nearby places may share values. Missing values remain unavailable. If you have respiratory or heart conditions, follow your clinician's advice and local official pollution alerts.
Map imagery shows historical Sentinel-2 satellite RGB data (EOX Cloudless 2016, CC BY 4.0). Weather and AQI markers are separate modelled data for geocoded cities, NOT temperatures or pollution measured from the satellite pixels. Use the layer switcher to view streets.
