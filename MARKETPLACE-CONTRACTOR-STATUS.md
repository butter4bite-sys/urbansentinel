## Activating a genuinely confirmed partner

1. Establish the contractor's actual identity, business name, permitted contact channel and service capacity.
2. Discuss referral terms and obtain explicit acceptance of being identified as an Urban Sentinel partner (ideally written and stored separately).
3. Open `access.html` → Owner Dashboard → **Marketplace Management**. Import/refresh the latest Pune listings if this is an existing website installation.
4. Edit the individual business entry. Check **Owner has checked business identity** and **I confirm that this business expressly agreed to be listed** only when true.
5. Enter that contractor's **actual agreed commission rate**, then enable tracked callback requests, save, and create its distinct business login.
6. Send a consent-based test enquiry, check unique referral ID, owner/contractor isolation, status updates and owner-confirmed completion before deploying publicly.

A **5% rate** in the demonstration calculator is an example only. Do not activate any provider, referral tracking or fee without genuine consent. The owner may archive or remove any listing. External phone numbers can change, so reconfirm before publishing.
