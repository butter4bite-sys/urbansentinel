Your information

Privacy overview

Know which information stays on your device, and what may be sent to external services.

Local assessment data

Cooling assessments, budgets, optional notes, completion checklists and thermometer logs are saved in your browser's localStorage. Without cloud sync you can use the site without identifying yourself. Clearing browser storage may delete the records.

Weather and map providers

When opening maps or fetching current weather, browsers contact EOX Cloudless, NASA GIBS, OpenStreetMap/Leaflet CDN servers and Open-Meteo, which may receive request metadata such as IP addresses. City searches and requests for solar/rainfall estimates also send the chosen city or map coordinates to the geocoding/forecast services under their own privacy policies. We don't upload your saved assessment fields to weather providers.

Owner/contractor portal and referral enquiries

No customer account is required. Owner and contractor login passwords are hashed and server-side sessions use HttpOnly cookies. When a customer voluntarily requests a callback, their name, telephone, optional email, city, service and enquiry notes are stored in a private server directory outside the public website folder. These are accessible to the owner and assigned contractor, not other contractors. Consent and submission time are recorded. No automatic third-party email is sent. Customers may request deletion from the website owner; the owner has a delete-enquiry control. Actual retention and contact arrangements must be published before public launch. All planning tools remain free. Browser-saved projects are not synced across devices.

Planning Assistant: The included rule-based assistant processes questions in your browser. There is no live Gemini/Netlify API call or paid AI quota used by this release.
Before a public launch: The operator must publish a working privacy contact, enquiry retention period, contractor data-sharing policy, and applicable legal notices before accepting real customer referrals. This page describes the included code, not a legal certification.
